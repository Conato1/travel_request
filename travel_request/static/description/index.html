The Expense Chaos

Manual email requests, untracked cash advances, lost paper receipts, and unmonitored travel expenses that exceed departmental budgets without warning — leading to painful month-end accounting reconciliations and audit discrepancies.

The XodetiStudio Solution

A unified, policy-driven travel ecosystem inside Odoo 18.0: automated budget reservation, structured 4-step approval chain, standard product cost lines, cash advance disbursement, and one-click GL journal settlement.

FEATURE 01 · REAL-TIME GOVERNANCE

Structured Request Form & Strict Budget Validation

Employees capture departure dates, destinations, transportation methods, and detailed itemized costs. The system actively checks available department budget balances before letting requests advance.

  • Hard Budget Intercept: Warns or blocks requests exceeding the allocated department limit.
  • Standardized Catalogs: Map standard travel costs (Airfare, Hotel, Meals) directly to expense products.
  • Cash Advance Tracking: Request advance funds directly linked to the trip profile.

FEATURE 03 · AUDIT READY

Official Travel Duty Orders & PDF Settlement

Generate professional print-ready Travel Orders (Surat Tugas) complete with employee credentials, destination schedule, approved budget breakdown, and formal signature blocks.

  • One-Click PDF Generation: Print directly from the approved travel request document.
  • Physical / Digital Signature Boxes: Ready for wet signatures or integration with Docu eSign.
  • Settlement Reconciliations: Compare budgeted advances against real receipts with automatic balance return.
